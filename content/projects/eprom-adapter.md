PCB adapter to eliminate the use of old 2708 and 2716 EPROM from Zaccaria 1st Gen CPU board.

Features:
- Support for 2764 and 27512 EPROM
- Up to 8 games selectable by dip-switch (with 27512)
- Direct replacement for IC1 socket

Includes free-play ROM patches for various Zaccaria games.
