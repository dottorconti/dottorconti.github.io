Modern remake of Zaccaria Pinball 1st Generation CPU board (1B1110/0-1) with improvements:

- Replace old EPROM 2708/2716 with 2764 or 27512
- Use AS6C6264 RAM instead of 2101/5101
- Mosfet array instead of CA3081
- Added bypass capacitors and protection

Full schematics and interactive BOM available at [dottorconti.net](https://www.dottorconti.net/flipper/zaccaria/zaccaria-cpu-1-generation-remake/).
